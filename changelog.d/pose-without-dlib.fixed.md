- Pose-guided enrollment no longer crashes on machines without `python3-dlib` (the common ArcFace + YuNet setup on most distros). Head pose now falls back to YuNet's 5 landmarks when dlib's 68-point predictor is absent, so `ward setup` / `ward enroll` complete instead of raising `RuntimeError: the 68-point landmarks (pose) needs python3-dlib`.
- ArcFace enrollment now captures IR models. The IR (primary) sample reached the embedding worker without its YuNet landmarks, so ArcFace alignment failed and every IR embed was dropped — enrollment produced RGB-only models and no dark-room unlock. The landmarks now travel with the sample.
- The dlib-less enrollment window is quicker and less fussy: a wider 14° arrival band for the noisier 5-point pose, sampling only while on target (no wasted embed-worker cycles), and up to two embedding jobs in flight so the worker is not idle between frames.
